# Good Dude John Cena Helped a High School Student Ask Her Special Needs Friend to Prom

John Cena is a 250-pound bodybuilder turned 16-time WWE Champion, but it turns out the biggest muscle in his body... is his heart. Well, no, that’s not scientifically accurate in any way. But still, this story of Cena helping a kindhearted high schooler ask a special needs friend to prom is the type of thing to make you believe in humanity again.

Earlier this week, Emily Blank, a student helper at Munford High School, took to Twitter to ask a favor of the famously good-willed grappler.

“[John Cena], I’m trying to ask a boy with special needs to prom and I need your help,” Blank wrote. “I know chances of you seeing this are slim but it’s worth a shot. Can y’all retweet this for my guy, Warren G?”

Cena—who also holds the world record for most wishes granted for the Make-A-Wish Foundation, by far—responded just a few days later, because of course he was going to.

“I’ve heard a lot about your wrestling career, and I will say, me being a 16-time champion, what you’ve done is quite impressive,” Cena said in the video, filmed courtesy of WMC Action News 5. “I’ve also heard there is a very special person who is possibly thinking of asking you a very important question. So Warren, the question is, will you see Emily at prom?”

What a pure dude. Cena, of course, is no stranger to gestures like this. There are the 300-plus wishes granted, yes, but chances are if you’ve heard Cena’s name during the latter half of his career, it’s because he either showed up somewhere to give a few fans a random act of kindness, or to be moved to tears himself listening to those he has inspired. Seriously, if you’re invited to participate in anything even vaguely related to John Cena, there’s a roughly 75% chance he pops out of a wall like the Kool-Aid Man just to give you a hug.

Between Cena and The Rock, it’s cool to know that the guys paid to punch each other in the face inside the ring are some of the best men alive outside of it.
