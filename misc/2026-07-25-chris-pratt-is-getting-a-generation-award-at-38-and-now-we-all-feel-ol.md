# Chris Pratt Is Getting a Generation Award at 38, and Now We All Feel Old

Let's face it: As much as we love Chris Pratt, Chris Hemsworth, Chris Pine, and Chris Evans, it can be hard to tell the Hollywood actors apart. Besides having the same first name, they all star in our favorite superhero and sci-fi movies; their fitness games are all on-point; if you blurred your eyes and looked at the four of them, you'd be hard-pressed to tell which Chris was which.

To help you keep track of everyone's favorite Chrises, we're rounding up what each of them got up to during the week. Welcome to This Week in Chrises.

Chris Pratt: Is getting a "Generation Award" at 38

Look, Chris Pratt is great — the guy makes us laugh and knows how to crush it in the gym. But when MTV announced earlier this week that it'll be honoring the Jurassic World actor with a Generation Award at the 2018 MTV Movie & TV Awards, we had a real problem with it. Why? Because the guy is only 38 years old! That's why.

As Variety noted, the award is typically given to someone who has made "significant contributions in film and television." Sure, Pratt is responsible for some real bangers like Guardians of the Galaxy and Zero Dark Thirty, and yeah he played a major role in NBC's Parks and Rec. OK fine, he totally deserves this award, but it's really making us feel old to call Pratt a generational icon.

Chris Evans: Got matching tattoos with his Avengers BFFs

Chris Evans may almost be done with playing Captain America, but that doesn't mean he's forgetting where he came from.

Evans, along with his fellow OG Avengers Scarlett Johansson, Robert Downey Jr, Chris Hemsworth, and Jeremy Renner, revealed they each got matching tattoos to commemorate their time together on screen. (The only original holdout was The Hulk, A.K.A. Mark Ruffalo.)

"Thanks for the ink, Joshua Lorde!(and for letting all of us maim you in return)," Evans shared in a tweet. "Love to @RobertDowneyJr @Renner4Real @chrishemsworth Scarlett (we just need to get Ruffalo blackout drunk)"

According to ComicBook, the matching tattoo is a stylized version of the Avengers logo, along with the number "6" to represent the six original Avengers. While these tattoos sound cool, we hope they won't come to regret them later.
