Warning: Spoilers ahead for Survivor: Edge of Extinction

We've come to expect the unexpected when it comes to Survivor―blindsides, secret alliances, and immunity idols seemingly pulled out of nowhere. But some of the best twists have come from the game itself, like the Survivor: One World season where both tribes lived on the same beach. This season, fans were thrilled to find out that eliminated players will have the chance to come back into the game after spending some time at an abandoned beach away from the rest of the castaways, called Edge of Extinction. This twist was revealed to the rest of the contestants on last night's episode, and they were understandably shocked.

The inhabitants of Extinction Island (Rick, Chris, Keith, Reem, Aubry, and Wendy) were then given a chance to return to the game, with the winner of the challenge heading back to join the main group. After Rick won, it seemed like the five other Extinction Island residents were set to go home, but it turns out the twist had another aspect: Rather than just being a first-half-of-the-game thing, Edge of Extinction is still in play, which means that the eliminated castaways have another chance to make it back into the game.

Additionally, any eliminated castaway who chooses to stay on Extinction Island after the first merge Tribal Council automatically earns a spot on the jury.
