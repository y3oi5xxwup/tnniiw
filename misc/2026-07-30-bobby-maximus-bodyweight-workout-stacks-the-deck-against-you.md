His most recent session falls right in line with this school of thought. In fact, the only piece of equipment you'll need isn't even something you'd typically find in the gym: a standard deck of playing cards. This is the same workout format that Maximus shared last week, due to popular demand—but now, there's a twist.

"Last week was a bit of a trial run," says Maximus. "We went through part of a deck of cards. This workout, we're going to go through the whole damn thing—and we're going to time ourselves to see how long it takes us."

Bobby Maximus' Clear the Deck Bodyweight Workout Challenge

[the Deck Against](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/)

The game is simple: Start your timer. Maximus (or you at home, if you ever want to replicate the format) decides on a bodyweight exercise, then pulls a card. Whatever number you pull, you follow up with that many reps. Make sure that you pick the move before you pull, so you're not encouraged to cheat. Keep going until the deck is toast.

Maximus finished the deck in 23:18. Can you go more quickly? Give it a try, and tell us how you did.
