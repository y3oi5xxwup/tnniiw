# This Mobility Session Will Help You to Nail the 90-90 Position

While the coronavirus (COVID-19) pandemic has halted the cadence of everyday life for just about everyone, the world is now beginning to adjust. We're all handling the challenges of social distancing and self-isolation differently—but that doesn't mean that we have to go it completely alone in every sense.

Dan Giordano, D.P.T. C.S.C.S., a Men's Health Advisory Board member and practicing physical therapist, hosted the latest session from his New York City apartment. The 20-minute mobility session aims to work the hips, lower extremity, and back.

[FarmHouse Fresh Wine Down](https://github.com/wrmmfz80fo/kvopwx/blob/main/product/2026-06-05-farmhouse-fresh-wine-down.md)

"We're going into the 90-90 position, which can be hard for people to get into," says Giordano. "It's a position that a lot of people do, but also a position that a lot of people struggle to get in." The position is exactly as the name implies: you're seated on your bottom, with both your legs bent at 90 degree angles.

Through the session, Giordano leads you into this tricky 90-90 position. All you need to take on the routine is a mat and some space to spread out.
