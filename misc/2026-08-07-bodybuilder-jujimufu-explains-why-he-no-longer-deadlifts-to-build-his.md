# Bodybuilder Jujimufu Explains Why He No Longer Deadlifts to Build His Back

YouTuber and bodybuilder Jujimufu, a.k.a. Jon Call, is currently in the process of preparing for a physique show, and part of that journey has involved cutting deadlifts out of his training regime. In a recent video, Juji breaks down why he is no longer deadlifting as a back-building exercise.

[to Build His](https://ageless-peptides.china-akan.com/)

"I've done two deadlift sessions in the last two and a half months, because my goal right now is to build muscle and prepare for a bodybuilding show, and that's not going to help me," he says, adding: "I've never got the best back pump in the world from deadlifting."

He recalls that he was a "monster" on squats and deadlifts when he was younger, but while they helped to grow his legs, his back and torso remained incredibly lean and slim. "I looked like I didn't work out, honestly," he says. These days, his back-shredding workout includes weighted pullups, bodyweight rows, and a rear delt and lat pulldown superset.

"We love deadlifting, we're not saying it's a bad lift or anything, we're just saying that for everyone's goals, it's not the right thing at the right time all the time," says Juji, who questions why this particular exercise is so venerated. "Why is deadlift this high thing for rating yourself as an athlete? Is the weighted chinup not a good measure of 'manliness' or 'machoness' or something?"

He also believes that deadlift training takes a psychological toll, which he wanted to avoid.

"You guys know that deadlift is the most emotional lift out of all of them," he says. "It's more emotional than squat, it's more emotional than bench press, it's more emotional than any other physical thing I can think of that has to do with strength, because for whatever reason, you know exactly where you are on it, and on a day by day basis it changes, and it just messes with your mind if you have a bad deadlift day, in my opinion... Getting that out of my life is going to help my focus on my goal."
