# 15-Minute Workout

This 15 minute workout offers a multi-muscle challenge that works your entire body and builds a stronger torso in only three exercises. So instead of spending hours in the gym, you’ll work more muscle in less time, which is also a key to burning fat. Complete the entire sequence, rest a minute, and then repeat the cycle as many times as you can in 15 minutes.

Dumbbell split squat:

Grab a pair of dumbbells and stand in a staggered stance, your left foot in front of your right and your arms hanging at your sides. Lower your body until your front thigh is parallel to the ground, making sure your upper body is upright and your lower back flat. Raise yourself back up into the staggered stance and complete a total of 8 repetitions. Switch legs and repeat.

Chin-up with knee-up:

Take an underhand grip on a chin-up bar with your palms facing your body. Pull your body up until your chest reaches the bar level. Pull your knees up to your chest, and then slowly lower your body back down. Let your knees drop slowly from your chest so that your body returns to the start position and repeat for 8 to 10 reps.
