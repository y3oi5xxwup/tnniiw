# Meet the 'Love Is Blind' Season 5 Cast

The pride and joy of Netflix is back. Love Is Blind is now in its fifth season, traveling around the country to try and find out if looks are everything in a relationship, while casting conventionally attractive people who promise to bring enough drama to make a bingeworthy season of television. Along with The Ultimatum, Netflix has made itself a powerhouse of reality dating television, outside of its scripted department.

While the show recruits a healthy bunch of men and women to fill the pods, there's only a few couples the show ends up focusing on. The first episode doesn't give that bias away, however, so it's worth getting to know the entire cast to see who could make up a potential starring couple.

And beyond the television drama, people really are invested in each couple's relationships. In Season 4, viewers stayed tuned in to how Jackie and Josh fared, along with Micah and Paul. Even after the show, fans take to social media to get the most up to date information on each person, paying attention to who they follow, who's in their photos, and what they're saying on Instagram.

If you're ready to dive back into the Love Is Blind world with your trusty hosts Nick and Vanessa Lachey, here's the newest cast of the series, along with a little bit of info about who each of them are, as TUDUM reports.
