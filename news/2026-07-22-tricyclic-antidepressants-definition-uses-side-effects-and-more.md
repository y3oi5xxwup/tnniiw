The Takeaway

Tricyclic antidepressants are a class of drugs that affect neurotransmitters, or chemical messengers, in the brain. They may be used to treat depression, migraine, chronic pain conditions, insomnia, and bedwetting in children. TCAs have an increased risk of suicidal thoughts, so be sure to talk to your doctor about the benefits and side effects before starting one.
