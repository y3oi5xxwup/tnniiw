# James Harrison's Resistance Workouts Are Too Much For Mere Mortals

An upper body day with exercises that seem as though one rep would dislocate your shoulder? Just another casual day in the gym for James Harrison.

The nearly 40-year-old NFL linebacker took to Instagram this morning to show off a bit of an upper body workout he recently did — including one exercise that should come with a sticker that says “don’t try this at home.” We certainly wouldn't expect anything less from the man who plays volleyball like this.

As you can see, this variation of a Z press involving weight plates attached to a flex bar with resistance bands even has the normally unshakable Harrison looking unsteady.

Needless to say, this is definitely not an exercise we advise doing if you haven't built up your form in the exercise first. Why? This extremely unstable exercise is just screaming potential injury. While Harrison’s clearly mastered the form, but he still works through the reps deliberately under the watchful eye of a spotter.

Unorthodox resistance exercises are kind of Harrison’s M.O. (And just, impossibly hard workouts in general — have you seen him push a sled?!) Check out how he ramped up the difficulty of a basic dumbbell bench press with a resistance band earlier this week. Harrison’s got great form and a strong base, but you might want to hold off on adding resistance to this exercise yourself, too. Make sure you have the form down first, and don't try this out without a spotter. Instead, join us in staring slack-jawed at his freakish strength.

That’s not to say there’s nothing you can pull from Harrison’s workouts. The other two exercises Harrison included in this latest onslaught of workout videos are unusually… doable. Tough as hell, but doable, once you've put a good amount of time in the weight room perfecting the basics.

Before you go all Harrison over those face pulls, make sure you understand the movement. Start out with the classic rope face pull with a weight you can control.

Despite the fact the vast majority of his exercises are literally impossible, there’s still much inspiration to pull from Harrison. The free agent just wrapped up his 16th season in the NFL, is a couple weeks from turning 40 years old and is obviously still looking fit as ever. Whoever ends up signing a contract with him will be gaining one strong dude, to say the least.
