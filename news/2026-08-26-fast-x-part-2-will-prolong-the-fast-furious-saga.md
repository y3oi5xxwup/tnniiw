# 'Fast X: Part 2' Will Prolong the 'Fast & Furious' Saga

The Fast & Furious saga began over 20 years ago and with its tenth (not including spinoffs) installment, Fast X, the high-octane adventure is finally beginning to end. But Fast fans needn't worry, because the finale to this larger-than-life action franchise obviously requires more than one movie to wrap up the stories of its many, many returning characters.

Vin Diesel, who has starred in the franchise since its very beginning, recently announced that a sequel to Fast X is in the works. "“After this explosive summer, I will have the privilege of coming to you for Part 2 in 2025," he said at CinemaCon 2023.

Fast X is now streaming on Peacock, so you can catch up with the most recent chapter in the long-running action epic from the comfort of your own home. And to tide you over until 2025, here's everything we know about Fast X: Part 2. And be warned; some spoilers follow for Fast X.

Who will direct Fast X: Part 2?

Fast X saw a changing of the guard behind the camera, with Louis Leterrier replacing franchise mainstay Justin Lin as director. Leterrier is expected to return to helm Fast X: Part 2, which Universal have described as a "companion piece" to Fast X.

Will there be another movie after Fast X: Part 2?

While this movie was ostensibly designed to close the book on the Fast saga, Diesel claims that the possibility of a third Fast X chapter has not yet been fully dismissed.

"Going into making this movie, the studio had asked if this could be a two-parter," Diesel said on the red carpet at the Fast X premiere. "And after the studio saw this, Part 1, they asked 'Could you make Fast X, the finale, a trilogy?'"
