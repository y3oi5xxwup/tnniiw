[in This](https://raazpeptide.com/category/fawe7)

Sam Elliott, the gravelly voice actor, is nominated for an Oscar this Sunday, Feb. 24, for the first time in his long career. He’s up for Best Supporting Actor for his turn as a tough-love administering older brother to Bradley Cooper in A Star is Born. Also nominated with Elliott: Sam Elliott’s legendary mustache. Congratulations to both!

The 74-year-old has enjoyed a career in film that’s spanned nearly 5 decades, and so has Sam Elliott’s Mustache. In that time it has gained a cult-like status in Hollywood. It’s the platonic ideal every man has in mind when he embarks on a Movember ‘stache growing mission. The voice and the mustache seem to speak together.

But what’s shocking is that Sam Elliott’s Mustache does not appear in every Sam Elliott movie. And because we’re in the service business here, we can tell you exactly which films (not including his television work) are lesser for lack of Sam Elliott’s Mustache. Now, when you’re at your Oscar party this Sunday, and the Best Supporting Actor category comes up and they call Sam Elliott’s name, you can impress everyone by telling them that, actually, Sam Elliott’s Mustache was not in We Were Soldiers.
